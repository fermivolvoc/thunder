Stella Unsealed Weapon, What do you pick?

Short answer: Gold Unsealed Weapon 

Read: This is my opinion so far. follow it at your own discretion. If you are not in a rush, DO NOT rush into things, it's always a good idea to wait to see what the majority thinks and decide from there.

Long Answer:

Why Gold Unsealed is stronger: (NEW GL weapon) (click me to view weapon info)

-No stacking mechanic so your first rotation will be a lot stronger + Faster with yellow weapon.

-Can get activated before even starting the maze with a simple jump.

-+15% more boss damage than red.

-Aerials do more damage. (haven't tested completely but Aerial 1 seemed weak).

Why Red Unsealed is weaker: (NEW GL weapon) (click me to view weapon info)

-Stacking Mechanic, First rotation will significantly be slower and do significantly less damage.

-Overall Damage, -15% boss damage is bad, even if you get 20% attackspeed, Gold Weapon also gives extra Aerial weapon Damage.

-Not much you can do with + 20% attackspeed Due to AB shortages besides adjust attackspeed mastery onto Critical damage. Or Run penetration into Valor.
